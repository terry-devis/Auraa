Auraa Habit Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Auraa is built as a private, local-first daily habit and ritual sanctuary. Your privacy is fundamental to our design—we do not require an account, and we do not collect, sell, or transmit your personal data to external servers.

1. Local-First Data Storage

All habits, completion logs, streaks, theme preferences, and profile details you enter in Auraa are stored exclusively on your device using local SQLite storage. We have no access to your habits or activity history.

2. Device Permissions We Use

  • Photos / Media Library (Optional): Only accessed when you choose to upload a custom profile picture (cropped and resized locally on your device) or save a Card Studio image to your phone's gallery. Images never leave your device unless you explicitly share them via your system share sheet.
  • Notifications (Optional): Used strictly to schedule local habit reminders, daily check-ins, and the 11:55 PM streak-saver alert directly on your device.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

Version 1.0.0 of Auraa does not include third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking, or cloud analytics. If future versions introduce optional ad-supported features, this policy and our Google Play Data Safety disclosures will be updated accordingly.

4. Data Deletion

Because all data lives locally on your device, you can delete all Auraa data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the app.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us via our repository at github.com/terry-devis/Auraa.
